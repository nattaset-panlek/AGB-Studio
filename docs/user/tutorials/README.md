# Tutorials

The five tutorials of Launcher · Learn. Each one is played in the Tutorial Steps panel (T-O13): a step has a short
text and one or more tasks, and a task ticks itself when you do it in AGB Studio. These pages list the same steps so
you can read them ahead or come back to them. Progress is kept between sessions.

| Tutorial | Time | What you learn | Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| Your first platformer | 15 min | Build Sky Hopper step by step | [first-platformer](first-platformer.md) |
| Scripting in C# for GBA | 20 min | fixed-point, Update(), OnTouch() | [csharp](csharp.md) |
| Making fonts with Thai | 10 min | variable width, vowels and tone marks | [thai-fonts](thai-fonts.md) |
| Palettes & VRAM | 10 min | why 16 colours, and how to fit more | [palettes](palettes.md) |
| Sound & music | 15 min | Maxmod, DMG channels, SFX Designer | [sound](sound.md) |

To start one, open the Launcher, choose the **Learn** tab and press **Start** (or **Continue**) on its row. In the
panel, **Back** and **Next step** move between steps and **Skip** marks the tasks of a step as done.
