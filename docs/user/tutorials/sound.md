# Sound & music

Maxmod, DMG channels, SFX Designer · 15 min · 3 steps

Music is played by Maxmod from MOD or XM modules. Sound effects are either samples (WAV) or effects made in the SFX
Designer, which play on the GBA's four DMG sound channels and need no sample data.

## 1. Open Audio

Music is MOD or XM, mixed by Maxmod; sound effects can use the DMG channels.

- [ ] Open Audio (Tools › Audio)

## 2. Design a sound

The SFX Designer makes retro sound effects without samples.

- [ ] Open the SFX Designer (Audio › Sound effects +)

## 3. Listen

Play the game and listen to the music and effects.

- [ ] Press Play
