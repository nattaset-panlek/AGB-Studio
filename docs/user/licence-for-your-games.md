# The licence of your games

This page says what licence applies to a game you make with AGB Studio and which notices travel with the ROM. It
describes the licences as decided for AGB Studio (decision D-05); it is not legal advice.

## Your content

The art, music, text, maps, data tables, dialogue and scripts you make are yours. AGB Studio claims no rights in them
and puts no conditions on how you use or sell them.

Template and sample projects are original work made for AGB Studio (decision D-20): their art, music and text are
generated by AGB Studio's own code, which is under the MIT licence, and contain no third-party material. D-05 does not
give them a separate licence.

## The engine inside your ROM

Every ROM contains the AGB Studio runtime (the C++ engine that AGB Studio compiles together with your game) and two
libraries from devkitPro:

| Part | Licence | What you must do |
|---|---|---|
| AGB Studio runtime | MIT | Keep its copyright and permission notice with copies of the game (text below) |
| libtonc (Tonc library by J Vijn) | MIT | Keep its notice; it is in `THIRD_PARTY.txt` |
| maxmod (music and sound, by Mukunda Johnson) | ISC-style | Keep its notice; it is in `THIRD_PARTY.txt` |

The MIT licence lets you use, change, publish and **sell** games that contain the runtime. There is no fee and no
royalty, and you do not have to show an AGB Studio logo. (The AGB Studio boot splash is a Project Settings option you
can turn off.)

## THIRD_PARTY.txt next to every ROM

Every build writes `THIRD_PARTY.txt` next to the ROM in the project's `build` folder, and Export ROM copies it into the
export folder next to the `.gba` file. It starts with "<your game title> was made with AGB Studio and contains the
following libraries" and holds the full libtonc and maxmod notices. When you share or sell the game, ship this file
with the ROM (for example in the same zip, or in the manual or credits).

## What is not covered here

- The C and C++ support libraries of devkitARM (newlib, libgcc, libstdc++) can also end up in the ROM. Their licences
  are those of devkitPro; the notices written next to the ROM do not list them.
- Fonts: a game font is built from the font file you choose. The default Thai font, Noto Sans Thai, is under the SIL
  Open Font License 1.1, which allows it to be embedded in a game you sell. For any other font, check its licence.
- "Game Boy Advance" and "GBA" are Nintendo trademarks. Describe your game as "for Game Boy Advance" if you need to,
  but do not use Nintendo's names or logos as your game's name or brand.

The full list of third-party components in AGB Studio itself (editor and toolchain) is in `docs/THIRD_PARTY.md`, and
the editor shows it under Help > About AGB Studio > Licenses.
